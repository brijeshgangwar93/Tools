Method. Back-aperture diameter D = 2·fobj·NA, where fobj = tube lens focal length / magnification. Matching lens focal length flens = f/#spectrometer × D, so the focusing cone into the slit fills the spectrometer's grating aperture without over- or underfilling it. This assumes your signal fills the objective's full NA and that the beam diameter is preserved through any relay (4f) stages between objective and coupling lens — real emitters (dipole-like sources, 2D materials, anything with restricted angular emission) often fill less than the full NA, so a beam diameter measured directly at the coupling lens position is more reliable than the back-aperture estimate. Use the override field above if you have a measured value.
