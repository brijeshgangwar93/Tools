Method. Paraxial Gaussian beam focusing: focused 1/e² radius wf = λf / (π·win), effective NA = win/f. Pinhole sizing follows the standard spatial-filter rule of thumb — a pinhole 1.3–1.6× the focused spot diameter passes ≈99% of the fundamental mode while rejecting higher-order content; smaller clips the fundamental mode itself, larger filters weakly. These are estimates for ideal Gaussian, diffraction-limited beams — real beam quality (M²), lens aberration, and alignment will reduce actual throughput below the geometric numbers shown here.
